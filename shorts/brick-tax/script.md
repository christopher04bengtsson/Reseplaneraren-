## Left out on purpose
- **The year of the double tax:** sources say 1801 or 1803, so the script gives no year.
- **The 1796/1797 rise to 5s and 5s 10d in 1805:** the dates disagree between sources, and 1805 would come after the crackdown in the timeline.
- **"Brick tax caused mathematical tiles":** disputed, because tiles were taxed too.
- **Dating buildings by brick size:** only one weak source.
- **No quotes from Wilkes or any real official.**

## Dramatisation
- The officer, the workers and the passers-by are invented characters. Wilkes is shown doing only what is documented: making big bricks.
- The pizza comparison is an explanation, not history.

## Wording to watch
- "Wilkes' gobs" is a historical nickname, said "WILKS-iz gobs".
- "Measham" is said "MEE-shum".
- "Shillings and sixpence" should be said slowly. On screen: "2s 6d".
