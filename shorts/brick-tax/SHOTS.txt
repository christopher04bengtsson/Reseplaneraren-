gobs | They became known as Wilkes' gobs, and a wall needed far fewer of them.
then | Then the government struck back. Any brick bigger than ten by five by three inches paid double.
so | So brickmakers shrank their moulds again, and the giant bricks stopped.
close | The tax lasted until eighteen fifty. But some of Wilkes' giant bricks are still standing today.
